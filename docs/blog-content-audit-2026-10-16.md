## Corrections

- Removed unsupported AppOmni survey percentages, millions-of-conversions statistics, editorial experience/audience credentials and user-research claims. Replaced them with practical, qualified explanations.
- Removed the assertion that browser processing automatically means no processing under privacy law; distinguished local document operations from website advertising/analytics traffic.
- Qualified conversion quality and privacy promises. Removed unsupported Word-versus-PDF security superiority and guaranteed SEO word-count outcomes.
- Replaced blanket legal acceptance claims in the signing guide with the actual distinction between a visible signature and certificate-based signing. Recipient requirements must be checked.
- Expanded five short guides (watermark, image resizing, units, postal PINs and currency) with specific examples, output checks, troubleshooting and limits. Random PIN digits are explicitly not a verified postal address. Currency numbers are clearly illustrative, not current rates.
- Added index/content completeness and duplicate redirect regression tests.

## Review limits

This is an inventory of all source articles and a targeted correction of identified quality/misrepresentation risks, not proof that every factual sentence is current or that content is original across the web. Word count is descriptive, not an AdSense minimum. No explicit adult/violent subject matter was identified in this targeted review; no categorical policy clearance is claimed.

Medical, tax, legal and financial articles still need topic-specific current-source verification before claiming expert review. Site-wide ads, consent/privacy disclosures, traffic quality, copyright provenance and the AdSense account’s own Policy Center are outside this content-only pass. Approval is Google's decision.

Google policies address low-value/replicated content and misleading representation; editing text does not by itself establish eligibility.
